For a proper triangle P, F(lambda,Xi)=G(P)+lambda*(Xi(P)-G(P)).
The 44 rational rows use normalized cyclic barycentric source weights, not unnormalized f=1+lambda*fX.
M_p minimizes the sum of the p-th powers of the distances to the three vertices.
