Tetrahedra with Orthogonal and Cevian Euler--Projection Properties

Edge-length classifications with orthocentric, jaw, equifacial, and diametral-mirror components

Ismail Hammoudeh · 30 August 2026 · Version 1.0.0 · 18 pages

Open PDF Download PDF Source and certificates

Abstract

For arbitrary faces, including equilateral ones, the orthogonal locus consists exactly of the orthocentric tetrahedra, the equifacial tetrahedra, and the tetrahedra with a four-cycle of equal edges whose faces are non-equilateral. If even one face is equilateral, either projection property forces the tetrahedron to be orthocentric. Cevian projection has the same ordinary branch and adds the non-equilateral diametral-mirror components.

The paper gives line-object definitions of both projections, proves the metric edge equations and their component decomposition, and explains the half-turn and reflection symmetries of the non-orthocentric branches. It is a first public release and is not peer reviewed.

Reproducibility files and checksums are maintained in the public GitHub repository. No DOI is claimed at present.
