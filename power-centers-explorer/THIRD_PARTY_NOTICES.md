# External mathematical formulas

The Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers (ETC) is the work of **Clark Kimberling and its named contributors**, hosted by the University of Evansville:

<https://faculty.evansville.edu/ck6/encyclopedia/etc.html>

Only 85 selected first barycentric-coordinate expressions are included in `data/etc-matches.json`. They are completed cyclically. The full ETC database is not redistributed. The formulas come from the supplied `ETC_X1_X72807.wxf` Triangle Center Tools snapshot, SHA-256 `ace501d40dd83785f89b4de8e4ddcc0c5081d82de82e1baf37816d10e547cbfa`, used previously in the Physically Inspired Triangle Centers project.
