What the draft studies

Physical laws and measurement protocols select points through equilibria, maxima, probability means, and singular limits. The manuscript organizes quantum, conformal, electrostatic, magnetic, thermal, fluid, mechanical, reaction, and optical constructions, including parameter families and exact classical reductions.

Accessible derivations are followed by numerical methods, individual contour atlases, Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers location comparisons, shape fingerprints, and vertex-motion attractivity evidence.

How to read the evidence The draft separates analytic certificates, validated finite computations, refined numerical observations, screens, and conditional constructions. Passing a finite screen is not presented as a global theorem.

The physical catalogue

Start with the classical realizations, then follow the result sheets through increasingly specialized physical selectors.

Production-removal, logistic population, darkness, fluorescence, inverse-square illumination, and solid angle.

A reference draft with a reproducibility companion

The manuscript is self-contained for definitions, derivations, and interpretation. Large coefficient arrays, sample records, executable verifiers, figures, checksums, and demonstrations belong to the companion archive.

Claims stay qualified

Some constructions have no convenient closed form; the draft makes no blanket transcendence or ETC nonmembership assertion. Uniqueness, branch conventions, dimensions, and limiting procedures remain part of each model.
