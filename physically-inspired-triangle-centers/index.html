What the atlas studies

Physical laws and measurement protocols select points through equilibria, maxima, probability means, and singular limits. The manuscript organizes quantum, conformal, electrostatic, magnetic, thermal, fluid, mechanical, reaction, and optical constructions, including parameter families and exact classical reductions.

Accessible derivations are followed by numerical methods, individual contour atlases, Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers location comparisons, shape fingerprints, and vertex-motion attractivity evidence.

How to read the evidence The atlas separates analytic certificates, validated finite computations, refined numerical observations, screens, and conditional constructions. Passing a finite screen is not presented as a global theorem.

Claims stay qualified

Some constructions have no convenient closed form; the atlas makes no blanket transcendence or ETC nonmembership assertion. Uniqueness, branch conventions, dimensions, and limiting procedures remain part of each model.

Reference manuscript

The atlas is prepared from the project’s draft and computational studies. Its title page leaves authorship and submission metadata unassigned. The lecture notes and explorer carry their own dated evidence and methods guidance.
