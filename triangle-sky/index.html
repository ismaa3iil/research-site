The Triangle Sky

Living research report · Version 0.2.0 · 71 pages

This living report develops the Triangle Sky: a framework in which triangle centers are lifted to tetrahedral apices and organized into triangle stars. It studies best-sphere-approximation and dihedral optimization stars, isotropic normal frames, hyperstars in higher dimensions, face-constrained tetrahedra, and the cascade of feet and traces that reproduce known and candidate centers in the Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers.

Pilot paper · Version 0.3.0 · 14 pages

The pilot collects the proved core of the Triangle Sky report and adds global optimization, power-strand, defect-decomposition, and boundary results. Its theorem claims are proved in the text, while the remaining open questions are explicitly identified.
